How it works

      
From the first message to the deploy.

      
No surprises. You know what you're getting, when and for how much before anything starts.

      
    
        
  1. 01 · briefing
    We talk on Direct
    You tell me the problem. I ask the right questions to understand your business.
    2. 
        
  2. 02 · proposal
    Clear proposal
    Scope, deadline and price in writing. You approve before a single line of code.
    3. 
        
  3. 03 · build
    Build in the open
    You follow the progress through a preview link and give feedback along the way.
    4. 
        
  4. 04 · deploy
    Live and handed over
    Published, tested on mobile, with the code and every access in your name.
    5. 
      

      
    
        
  • The code is yoursNo lock-in. Repository, domain and accounts in your name.
    • 
        
  • Mobile firstBuilt for the phone, where your customers actually are.
    • 
        
  • Accessible and fastReadable, keyboard friendly and light to load.
    • 
        
  • Direct lineYou talk to the person who writes the code.
